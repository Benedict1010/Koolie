## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional-grade, friction-free environment for on-demand service fulfillment. Built for a multi-sided marketplace connecting homeowners and property managers with verified local trade professionals, the aesthetic balances modern SaaS rigor with tactile, service-oriented warmth. 

The core visual language relies on **Modern Corporate Minimalism with Micro-Tactile Precision**:
- **Confidence and Security:** High-density structural clarity, calm deep-indigo anchors, and unambiguous state feedback eradicate cognitive friction during high-stress domestic repairs (e.g., burst pipes, power outages).
- **Service Verification:** Trust markers are treated as first-class architectural citizens rather than decorative stickers—verified trades badges, background-check indicators, and liability insurance status receive dedicated optical weight.
- **Frictionless Utility:** Clean white canvas surfaces, disciplined 1px hairpins, razor-sharp typography hierarchies, and intentional status accents create an agile, premium platform suitable across field mobile devices, responsive dispatch tablets, and multi-tenant admin consoles.

## Colors

The palette leverages a calibrated dark-slate and cobalt hierarchy paired with high-clarity semantic accents. Color is strictly functional, conveying trust, workflow state, and immediate validation.

### Structural Anchors
- **Primary Canvas & Text (#0F172A / #1E293B):** Midnight slate anchors primary headers, navigation shells, high-priority buttons, and critical modal dialogs.
- **Brand Interactive Accent (#2563EB / #1D4ED8):** Electric cobalt guides user interaction, flow advancement, active booking progress, and selected states.
- **Base Canvas (#F8FAFC & #FFFFFF):** Off-white base field paired with pure white container surfaces prevents optical fatigue and heightens perceived interface speed.
- **Neutral Boundaries (#E2E8F0 & #CBD5E1):** Low-contrast structural borders provide crisp perimeter containment without visual clutter.

### Semantic & Status Engine
- **Verified / Confirmed (#059669 / #10B981):** Emerald accent dedicated strictly to verified credentials, background-clear badges, instant payments, and active worker statuses.
- **Pending / Attention Required (#D97706 / #F59E0B):** Deep warm amber signaling quote evaluations, pending approvals, and scheduled arrival intervals.
- **Critical / Interrupted (#DC2626):** Crimson accent reserved for service cancellations, payment failures, dispute flags, and critical system alerts.

### Role Identifiers
- **Customer View:** Focused around `#2563EB` primary actions for streamlined search, scheduling, and approvals.
- **Professional View:** Emerald and slate accents emphasize income tracking, job claims, routing, and availability switches.
- **Admin View:** High-density indigo layout with dual-color audit indicators for system-wide verification tasks.

## Typography

The typographic engine marries **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headers with **Inter** for dense transactional UI.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** injects subtle geometric energy and warmth into page titles, category headings, and metric counters without compromising structural poise. Tight negative tracking (-0.01em to -0.03em) prevents large titles from drifting loose.
- **Inter** handles data tables, quote line items, field inputs, badges, and long-form reviews. Its tall x-height, neutral aperture, and legible numeric tabular spacing support seamless reading on small screens under varied field-lighting conditions.
- **Numeric Discipline:** All pricing, ratings, and time-tracking components must apply tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across order summaries and dispatch schedules.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8pt rhythmic layout architecture** across a fluid 12-column responsive grid.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 fluid columns, `16px (1rem)` gutters, `16px (1rem)` safe canvas margins. Single-column stacked workflows. Bottom sheets replace complex modals.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 8 fluid columns, `24px (1.5rem)` gutters, `24px (1.5rem)` page margins. Service categories and provider lists transition to 2-column cards.
- **Desktop (1024px - 1440px):** 12 fluid columns constrained to a max-width of `1280px`, `24px (1.5rem)` gutters, and `32px (2rem)` outer margin.
- **Wide Display (> 1440px):** Max container capped at `1400px` centered, ensuring that multi-pane dispatch sheets and admin consoles maintain focus and prevent scan line drift.

### Spatial Discipline
All margins and paddings snap to multiples of 4px and 8px:
- `space-xs` (8px) for badge inner padding, item list spacing, and micro-actions.
- `space-md` (16px) for form control field gaps and standard card padding.
- `space-lg` (24px) for card body internal padding and intra-section stack distancing.
- `space-xl` (32px) and `space-2xl` (48px) for modular layout blocks and section-level separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp 1px hairpins alongside diffused, ambient micro-shadows. Flat bright white planes float slightly over a cool slate base (`#F8FAFC`), establishing hierarchy without dramatic drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat Canvas - `#F8FAFC`):** System background surface. No elevation, no border.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface - `#FFFFFF`):** High-density standard card surfaces, listing items, and form containers. Defined by a crisp 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0` backed by an ambient micro-shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover):** Lifted cards, sticky navigation bars, and active provider tiles. Supported by a dual-cast shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` combined with `#CBD5E1` border transitions.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays, Flyouts & Drawers):** Dropdown select panels, context menus, and role switcher sheets: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
5. **Level 4 (Modals & Emergency Confirmations):** Critical booking confirmation prompts and credential uploaders: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` over a 40% `#0F172A` backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a calibrated **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry to balance modern tech authority with friendly consumer warmth:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Standard Cards):** Standardized at `0.5rem (8px)` corner radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Feature Cards & Container Panels:** Elevated to `0.75rem (12px)` or `1rem (16px)` (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) to clearly group complex sub-flows (e.g., job checklists, technician bios).
- **Badges, Filters & Status Pills:** Full circular caps (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between actionable structural cards and scannable status pills.
- **Icons & Avatar Shields:** Verified technician avatars use `rounded-full` with an offset emerald circular badge at the bottom-right corner.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Now, Confirm Job):** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight 600, radius `8px`. Hover: `#1E293B`. Focus: `ring-2 ring-offset-2 ring-[#2563EB]`.
- **Secondary Action (View Profile, Details):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Direct Interactive (Call Pro, Accept Dispatch):** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E40AF`.
- **Destructive/Cancel:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#DC2626`, border `1px solid #FEE2E2`. Hover: `#FEE2E2`.
- **Sizes:** Small (32px height, 12px font), Medium (40px height, 14px font), Large (48px height, 16px font).

### 2. Status Badges & Pill Indicators
- **Verified Pro Badge:** Pill-shaped, background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`. Includes a 12px checkmark icon.
- **In-Progress / Dispatched:** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E40AF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`.
- **Pending Review / Awaiting Estimate:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Emergency Service Tag:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`.

### 3. Cards (Service & Professional Profiles)
- Standard service and provider cards feature a solid `#FFFFFF` background, a `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, and an 8px radius.
- Padding: 16px on mobile, 20px on desktop.
- Sub-elements include technician avatar with emerald trust ring, primary trade category, hourly or flat rate with tabular numbers, average rating star count (`#D97706`), and completed jobs counter.
- Hover state smoothly elevates shadow to Level 2 and transitions border color to `#CBD5E1`.

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Standard Input:** Height 44px, radius 8px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border `#2563EB`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`, outline none.
- **Validation (Error):** Border `#DC2626`, text `#DC2626`, focus ring `#DC2626` at 15% opacity, supporting error icon and message positioned below input.
- **Validation (Success):** Border `#059669`, with an inline check icon inside the right edge.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** 18px square, 4px corner radius, `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state uses `#2563EB` fill with a crisp white check vector.
- **Radio Button:** 18px circle, checked state displays `#2563EB` ring with a 6px centered dot.

### 6. Role Switchers & Tabs
- Segmented control pills set inside a `#F1F5F9` tray with 6px internal padding.
- Active tab features a pure white elevated pill (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E2E8F0`, subtle micro-shadow, and `#0F172A` text.
- Inactive tabs remain unbordered with `#64748B` text, changing to `#0F172A` on hover.

### 7. Empty States & Onboarding Panels
- Centered layout containing an icon encapsulated inside a soft circular container (`#F1F5F9`), followed by a `Plus Jakarta Sans` 18px semi-bold headline and a max-40-character description in `Inter` 14px slate (`#64748B`).
- Clean primary action button immediately below the description (e.g., "Request an Electrician", "Complete Your Certification").
